# Influencer Pricing Research — Jobright Partnership (July 2026)

Context: Jobright reached out for a partnership. Current quote: **$1,000 for 4 videos**,
including usage rights, repost (whitelisting/Spark Ads) rights, and a collaborative
invite link. That's an effective **$250/video, all rights included**.

## Verdict: this is underpriced

$250/video *all-in* is UGC-tier pricing (content the brand uses but you don't post).
But these are in-feed videos posted to your own audience, **plus** ad usage rights,
**plus** repost/collab distribution — each of which normally carries its own premium.

## 2026 market benchmarks

| Item | Typical rate |
|---|---|
| TikTok in-feed video, nano (1K–10K followers) | $50–$300 base |
| TikTok in-feed video, micro (10K–100K followers) | $200–$1,500 base |
| UGC video (no posting) | $150–$300 (avg ~$212) |
| Usage rights (paid ads / brand reuse) | **+30–100%** on base |
| Spark Ads / whitelisting / repost | **+25–100%** on base |
| Bundle discount for 3–6 videos | −10–25% (not more) |
| Niche premium (tech/career/finance) | up to 2–3× lifestyle rates |

Sources: Influee, Insense, Modash, Shopify, Social Native, inBeat, InfluenceFlow,
Launchpoint, Stan (2026 pricing guides).

## What the current quote leaves on the table

1. **Usage + repost rights are being given away.** Perpetual, unrestricted usage
   normally *doubles* a rate. Standard practice is 30/60/90-day licenses with
   renewal fees.
2. **Bundle discount is too deep.** 4 videos should discount ~10–20% off the
   single-video rate, not compress everything to UGC pricing.
3. **Brand history isn't priced in.** Chegg, Zipcar, and Adobe partnerships
   justify the upper half of any tier's range.
4. **Career/tech niche pays a premium** — Jobright's audience (job seekers,
   students, early-career professionals) is high-value and hard to reach.

## Suggested pricing structure

Pick the row matching current follower count / avg views:

| Tier | Base per video | 4-video bundle (−15%) | + usage & repost (90 days, +50%) | Package ask |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ~5K followers | $250 | $850 | +$425 | **~$1,300** |
| 10–25K followers | $400 | $1,360 | +$680 | **~$2,000** |
| 25–75K followers | $600 | $2,040 | +$1,020 | **~$3,000** |

Alternative if Jobright's budget is truly capped at $1,000: keep the price but
cut the rights — 4 videos with **organic repost only, 30-day license, no paid
ads/whitelisting**; paid usage available as a +50% add-on.

## Negotiation notes

- Don't open with "I'm flexible on rate" — it invites the low anchor. Anchor
  high with a rate card and let them counter.
- Time-limit all usage rights; renewals are recurring revenue.
- Since Jobright wants a long-term partnership, offer a **monthly retainer**
  (e.g., 2 videos/month at a modest discount) instead of discounting a one-off
  bundle — recurring income justifies a better per-video price for them.
- Typo to fix in the outreach reply: "collabroative" → "collaborative".
